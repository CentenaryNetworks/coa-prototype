About Chiefs of Angels

Above the angels

The name Chiefs of Angels was coined from the Biblical reference to human beings’ spiritual ranking and authority.

We believe humanity has been made superior to angels, so we should wear clothing that agrees with that truth.

Our clothing is a mix of up-cycled, recycled, repurposed and newly made pieces. Bringing those ways of making together with a rock ’n’ roll sensibility is what sets Chiefs of Angels apart from other brands.

The leather jacket is the label’s core style. Everything else is classic clothing, always with a twist, made to complement that timeless jacket. Our clients describe the result as spiritual, rebellious and timeless.

What the brand stands on

Three pillars

Rock ’n’ roll history

The sound, the attitude and the leather jacket. Rock ’n’ roll shapes the silhouettes, the graphics and the way every piece is worn.

Spirituality

Clothing as a reminder of who you are. We want people to feel empowered, even invincible, in what they wear.

Sustainability

Fashion leaves a heavy footprint on the planet. We work with up-cycled, recycled and repurposed materials, and do what we can to reduce our own impact.

An international African contemporary luxury brand.Where Chiefs of Angels is heading

Our aim is to stand alongside the top brands in every fashion capital. We keep improving how we work to meet international standards, while still meeting the exacting taste of the global citizen.

We want Chiefs of Angels to be known for quality, attainability and uniqueness, without compromising the pillars the brand is built on.

Services

What we make

High-quality clothing for all people, made with the quintessential rock ’n’ roller in mind.

We believe clothing is the most immediate form of creative expression. That idea guides every collection: pieces that express your thoughts, your lifestyle and your spirituality.

Quality materials are chosen for every production run. Chiefs of Angels doesn’t compete with other brands. We’ve set our own standards and our own unconventional path in fashion.
